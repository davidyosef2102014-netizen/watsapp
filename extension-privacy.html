BIRGSOL AI — Browser Arm

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 2026

What this extension is

The “BIRGSOL AI — Browser Arm” extension is a remote arm for the BIRGSOL AI web app. When you give a command inside the BIRGSOL app, the extension reads the page you are currently on and performs the actions you request — clicking, typing, and scrolling. It has a single purpose: letting the BIRGSOL app act in your browser on your behalf. It has no chat, no login, and no account of its own.

What data it accesses

To carry out your command, the extension reads the content of the active tab (such as visible text, links, and a screenshot of the page) and the elements it needs to click or type into. This happens only when you trigger an action from the BIRGSOL app.

How that data is used

  • The page content is sent only to your own BIRGSOL app session, so the AI can understand the page and decide the next action.
  • It is used solely to complete the task you asked for.
  • The extension does not store this data, does not keep a history, and does not send it to any BIRGSOL server of its own.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose unrelated to the single purpose above, and never for creditworthiness or lending.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — read the current page and perform the click / type / scroll you requested.
  • tabs — open or navigate to a site when you ask the agent to.
  • host access (all sites) — because you may ask the agent to act on any website you choose.
  • alarms — automatically disable the extension after a short period of inactivity, as a safety measure.

Your control

The extension only acts on your explicit command from the BIRGSOL app, and automatically disarms when idle. You can remove it at any time from your browser’s extensions page, and all access ends immediately.
